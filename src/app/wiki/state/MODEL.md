# EDU Living State Observatory / 三相動態観測所

Edutext is a private canonical narrative source. The exported registry contains only fictional names/IDs and qualitative context, not numerical traits, historical assets or dates. Snapshot source: 53d8845b578d6d64dc74d070f224dcb908c3ff13. No private API token is embedded or fetched by browsers. The existing 1688 E528 residents plus 18 explicitly contemporary legacy entries are eligible for the model. Other legacy characters remain searchable but no current metrics are invented. Registry order is stable; changing roster or seed defines a new simulation version. Refresh the nonnumeric snapshot when the roster changes.

State x=(h,w,a), with health and social authority bounded and wealth in annual essential-basket units. Shared initial condition (0.65,1,0.3), 180-day burn-in, seeded Gaussian and jump noise. Daily synchronous transitions exchange graph messages using exp(q dot x_neighbor), normalized attention; shared q updates from transition residuals (adaptive heuristic, not a trained ML model). h responds to care, capital, authority, peers, pressure and genotype/environment expression; w responds to wage, expense, health, reputation, credit and stochastic shocks; a responds to peers, health, wealth and pressure. All axes interact in both directions; no personal coefficient or preassigned rank. Net wealth=assets-liabilities; economy is open, not a closed conservation model. 10M n per essential annual basket is an explicit uncalibrated model assumption. Wealth index is 0.5+atan(w/5)/pi, not a percentile or permanent ability. Top share denominator is total positive net wealth.

Six hypothetical diploid loci, anonymous founding cohort, 16 generations of finite-population sampling, migration, recombination and mutation; no asserted actual biological parents or specific genes. Genotype remains stable during the simulated lifetime; expression depends on health and environment. Weak context-dependent viability selection operates only in anonymous ancestral cohorts at one hypothetical locus, in addition to drift. Allele advantage reverses with environmental pressure; this is an uncalibrated fictional model, not a genetic hierarchy or human fitness score. No selection on identity or wealth. Identity and name do not enter dynamics. Environment includes modeled care and opportunity, fictional region effects and canon 197-day planet / 29-year binary cycles. These are analogies to stochastic non-equilibrium and coupled fields; not a derivation from quantum gravity, not a medical prediction. Charts include the final 28 burn-in days so the first model day has a visible history. Current model uses UTC elapsed days from 2026-11-27, E528 day 1; stops at E528 end. Reform and crisis are counterfactuals, not canon events. No external inference service or paid API.

## Version 3 — inspectable effective theory, not a theory of fundamental physics

Let x=2h-1, y=tanh(w/5), z=2a-1. For the symmetric matrix M with zero diagonal and off-diagonal elements x,y,z, Tr(M^3)=6xyz exactly. Define V=g xyz + lambda (x^4+y^4+z^4)/4, g=.018, lambda=.008. Implement cubic and quartic gradient components in normalized coordinates as phenomenological drift contributions to H,W,A (scaled by 1/2 for H/A, and W uses a chosen mobility, not the exact pullback metric of tanh). All coordinates are dimensionless except W in basket units. V is bounded below as a polynomial on R^3 for lambda>0; the full open stochastic model is not gradient flow, does not conserve V, and is not proven stable by V. The cubic vertex vanishes on x=0 or y=0 or z=0; its individual derivatives need not vanish there. This is an imposed factorization, NOT a hidden zero of a scattering amplitude. The direct pairwise panel excludes cubic and indirect terms; cubic drift is shown separately.

Empirical connected three-point cumulant = E[(h-Eh)(tanh(w/5)-E tanh(w/5))(a-Ea)]. It is not itself proof of irreducible causal interaction. Binary threshold partition gives eight octants, probability p; entropy H=-sum p ln p; total correlation=sum marginal H-H(p). Dropping the authority bit defines a deterministic coarse-graining; D_KL(p||uniform8)>=D_KL(Cp||uniform4) by log-sum inequality. No holographic duality is asserted. Empirical one- and two-day transition kernels from the last 28 burn-in days through the selected day are compared via weighted TV distance between P_2 and P_1^2; nonstationarity, hidden state and finite sampling contribute. Rows without sufficient support are excluded and sample counts exposed. This is a closure diagnostic rather than a bootstrap solution or proof of locality. Time steps and finite population are regulators; no UV completion or continuum-limit claim.

Online shared attention: logits q dot f_j, softmax messages m=sum_j alpha_j f_j, auxiliary forecast x+.15(m-x), MSE against realized next normalized state with stop-gradient target; exact local derivative dm/dq=weighted covariance of features. Projected SGD with shared q; no personal fitted coefficients, no external training or out-of-sample validation. The training MSE is not real predictive accuracy. Model v3 replays different dynamics from v2.

## Version 4 — numerically consistent SDE, exact gradient vertex, closed ledger, estimator-grade diagnostics

Numerics. Health and authority follow the target Itô SDE dp = mu dt + 4 sigma p(1-p) dW (sigma = .025 for h, .018 for a; equals the v3 noise at p = 1/2 and vanishes at the boundary). They are integrated in u = logit p with the exact Itô drift mu/(p(1-p)) - 8 sigma^2 (1-2p), tamed as f/(1+|f|/4) (Hutzenthaler–Jentzen–Kloeden 2012), dt = 1 day. States stay in (0,1) without clipping, so no artificial boundary mass is created. Tests check first-order mean and variance consistency by deterministic quadrature over the Gaussian increment, and boundedness under extreme drift/noise. Genotype summaries are precomputed once per lifetime. Attention messages no longer allocate per neighbor (about 15% faster).

Vertex. The drift is now the exact gradient flow x_k' = -Gamma_k dV/dx_k in physical coordinates with constant mobility Gamma = diag(1/4, 5, 1/4), including the tanh pullback (1-y^2)/5 for w that v3 omitted. Hence the vertex sub-flow satisfies dV/dt = -sum Gamma_k (dV/dx_k)^2 <= 0 (reported as dissipation; a test verifies the finite-difference gradient and monotone descent). The full open stochastic model is still not a gradient system.

Flow-of-funds ledger. Per-capita cumulative wages, essential costs, multiplicative returns, upside/downside compound-Poisson jumps, peer exchange, debt service, vertex drift and boundary clipping are recorded from t = -28. The identity  Δ mean net wealth = sum of flows  is checked every run (residual ~1e-13). This makes the open economy auditable: e.g. adverse jumps exceed upside jumps in expectation, which is why the median net wealth is negative; this is a stated model property, not canon.

Nonequilibrium. With consecutive 8-bin states, the time-reversal asymmetry KL(p_ij || p_ji) (Jeffreys pseudocount 1/2) is reported in nat/day; under stationarity it is a lower bound on entropy production of the observed coarse process (data processing), and nonstationarity may bias it. The largest net probability currents are listed. The empirical one-step kernel on supported bins yields its stationary law pi (power iteration), TV distance of the current distribution to pi, and the second eigenvalue |lambda_2| via Gelfand's formula with the ratio (||D^512||/||D^256||)^(1/256), D = P - 1 pi^T, giving relaxation time tau = -1/ln|lambda_2|. A two-state test recovers lambda_2 = .7. Typical tau (~130–310 days) exceeds the 180-day burn-in: the observatory explicitly shows that the cohort is still relaxing, not at stationarity.

Tails and inequality. Gross-asset Gini (sorted closed form) and the Hill tail index of positive net wealth on k = max(5, floor(sqrt(n+))) order statistics with asymptotic SE alpha/sqrt(k) (tested on exact Pareto quantiles). Plug-in entropy also has the Miller–Madow correction. A nonparametric bootstrap over agents (B = 200, independent stream that never perturbs the dynamics) gives a 95% percentile interval for the connected third cumulant; it measures finite-population resampling uncertainty, not seed-to-seed Monte Carlo variance or causal structure.

Population genetics. Per generation: Nei's G_ST across regions (ratio of averages over loci, no small-sample correction), expected heterozygosity H_e, and mean adjacent-locus r^2 from phased haplotypes. Values describe the fictional anonymous ancestral cohort only.
